How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
  • Move your tokens clockwise around the board and up your coloured path to the centre.
  • Land on an opponent to send them back to base. ⭐ Stars and start squares are safe.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing, or getting a token home earns another roll — but three 6s in a row lose your turn.
  • You need the exact number to reach home. First to get all four tokens home wins!
  • 🎯 Easy sixes: while all your tokens are at home, 6s come much more often — you'll be out within 3 rolls.
  • 💎 Gems: you get 5 per game. Don't like your roll? Spend a gem to roll again.
  • 🎁 Stickers: tap another player to throw a rose, a bomb, a tomato and more!
